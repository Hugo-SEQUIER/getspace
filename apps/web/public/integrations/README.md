# Ecosystem brand assets

These marks identify the integration targets illustrated in the landing-page hero. They do not indicate partnerships or verified compatibility. The public connection guide records the currently verified client.

- ChatGPT / OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Z.ai, Meta, MiniMax, and Kimi: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons), distributed under the included `LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt`.
- Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, OpenCode, Cline, and Devin: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons), under the same included license.
- PrimeAgent: [the official Prime Agent butterfly mark](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-agent/blob/main/assets/brand/prime-butterfly-black.svg), with `LICENSE-prime-agent.txt`.
- Factory (Droid): [the official Factory favicon](https://factory.com/favicon.svg).
- Pi: [the Pi website logo](https://pi.dev/logo-auto.svg), referenced by the [official coding-agent README](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono/tree/main/packages/coding-agent). Repository license retained in `LICENSE-pi.txt`.
- Hermes: `hermes-portrait.png` is the picture supplied by the user on 18 September 2026, used unchanged at their request. It replaces the previous official favicon in the hero; the original SVG and its `LICENSE-hermes.txt` remain in this directory.
- OpenClaw: [the official OpenClaw favicon](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/ui/public/favicon.svg), with `LICENSE-openclaw.txt`. Declarative animation was removed for a static, motion-safe diagram; the mark is otherwise unchanged.

Assets were retrieved on 18 September 2026 and are served locally. Brand names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
